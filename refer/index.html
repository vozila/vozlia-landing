How you get paid

  • The shop or barber gets a 14-day free trial (no card), then 20% off for as long as they stay.
  • Barbershop: $200. $50 each month the shop pays, for its first 4 months.
  • Barber who rents a chair: $150. $50 each month the barber pays, for their first 3 months.
  • Your first payment is handed to you in person. After that, we pay by Zelle once a month, so you'll need a bank account with Zelle.

The rules

  • One referral per shop: the shop is credited to the link it signs up with.
  • You can't refer a shop you own or work at.
  • You must tell the owner you get paid.
  • Paying owners to sign up, faking a shop, or a disputed or refunded payment ends that shop's payments.
  • We may change or end this program with 30 days' notice; payments you've already earned are still paid.
