Get paid to show NYC barbershops Join The Line

Join The Line lets clients join a barbershop's walk-in line from the window sign, Google or Yelp, wait wherever they like, and get a text when they're up. We're looking for people in New York City to visit shops, show the owner a one-minute demo, and book them a time to talk with us.

What you'll show shop owners (1 min)

  • Your own territory. Suggest the barbershops in your neighborhood. Once we approve them, they're yours for 14 days, and you pick the order.
  • We print you a demo poster for each shop. Let the owner try the one-minute demo, then leave the poster and a card that explains what happens next.
  • Your goal on every visit: book the owner a time to talk with us. We call them then, answer their questions and help them get set up.
  • In shops where barbers rent their chairs, each barber is their own boss. Leave one poster for the shop and a sign-up card for each barber. If a barber isn't in, leave their card at their chair.
  • Before you leave each shop, fill in a short visit report on your phone (below).
  • Independent contractor: set your own hours. In person in NYC; training is online.

What you earn

  • $20 per quality visit, whether or not the owner signs up.
  • A quality visit: a real conversation with the owner or a barber, who tries the demo; your visit report filed on the spot, with a storefront photo; a time for us to call the owner, or a clear "not interested" and why; and the poster left with someone who'll use it.
  • A drop-off with no conversation isn't paid. That shop goes on your revisit list, and a quality visit later pays the full $20.
  • One paid visit per shop, however many barbers you talk to. If another rep already visited a shop, a second visit isn't paid.
  • Your first route pays up to 8 shops. More routes open up from there.
  • Top performers may be offered higher pay or account-manager work (a possibility, not a promise).
  • Paid by Zelle within 2–3 business days, once we've verified your leads.

Your visit report: filled in on the spot

Before you leave each shop, fill in your visit report on your phone. You get the form when you start. We check every item before we pay, so please answer them all.

  • A photo of the storefront.
  • Do the barbers rent their chairs, work on commission, or a mix?
  • How many chairs the shop has.
  • Which barbershop apps they use: Booksy, Square, Vagaro, StyleSeat, another, or none.
  • Are they interested in our AI receptionist? In a window sign?
  • A time for us to call the owner, set together with them. If they're not interested, why.
  • Owner agreed to a call: tick it only with the owner right there. Only then do you add their name and contact details.
  • In shops where barbers rent their chairs: how many barbers work there, which of them took a sign-up card, and a barber's contact details only with that barber's OK.
  • Barbers who weren't in: the cards you left at their chairs, and each one's name, the Instagram posted at their chair, and their usual days, if you know them. Never take an absent barber's phone number from a coworker.

Ask before you take photos inside, and keep customers' faces out of the shot.

How you start

  1. A short video interview on Google Meet, with a two-minute role-play: pitch us as if we're the barber.
  2. A paid field test: 3–4 shops we pick, within a week. Then we review how it went.
  3. Your W-9, after your first week, in person or through a secure form. Never send tax information by email.
  4. Your first 8 paid shops. More routes open up from there.
