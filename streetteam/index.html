Get paid to show NYC barbershops Join The Line

Join The Line lets clients join a barbershop's walk-in line from the window sign, Google or Yelp, wait wherever they like, and get a text when they're up. We're looking for people in New York City to visit shops, show the owner a one-minute demo, and book them a time to talk with us.

  • Visit barbershops in the neighborhoods you pick.
  • Show a one-minute demo on your phone.
  • Your goal on every visit: book the owner a time to talk with us. We call them then, answer their questions and help them get set up.
  • After each visit, send us a short visit report (below).
  • Set your own hours. In person in NYC; training is online.
  • Independent contractor. Paid visits start after an in-person interview with us and a short online training.

Applying is free. We'll never ask you for money, and this form never asks for your Social Security number or bank details.

What you earn

  • $20 for each shop visit we verify, whether or not the owner signs up.
  • $50 bonus when that shop becomes a paying customer, paid after its first paid month.
  • One paid visit per shop. If another rep already visited a shop, a second visit isn't paid.
  • Paid within 24 hours of your visit, once we've checked your visit report.

Your visit report: what we need from every visit

After each visit, text or WhatsApp us your report at (732) 588-3540 (WhatsApp). We check every item before we pay, so please answer them all.

  • Photos of the outside and the inside of the shop.
  • The shop's name.
  • The shop's WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook page.
  • The name of the person you talked to.
  • Do the barbers rent their chairs (booth rental) or work on commission?
  • Where the shop advertises.
  • The owner's name and contact details, if you know them.
  • Which barbershop apps they use (for example Booksy, Squire or Square).
  • How many chairs the shop has.
  • Did you book a time for the owner to talk with us? If yes: the day, the time, and who we'll be talking to.
  • If you didn't book a time: can we come back in 6 months?

Ask before you take photos inside, and keep customers' faces out of the shot.
