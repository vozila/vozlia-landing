AI Receptionist

How does an AI receptionist work for a barbershop?

The short answer

An AI receptionist answers your shop's phone for you — day or night — and books, reschedules, or cancels the appointment by talking to the caller, then puts it on your calendar. For a barbershop it also runs the walk-in line, so the chair keeps moving even when both your hands are busy with a client.

What it actually does when the phone rings

When a call comes in and you can't pick up — you're mid-fade, it's after hours, the shop is slammed — the AI answers on the first couple of rings instead of sending the caller to voicemail. It talks like a normal front-desk person: it finds an open slot, confirms the service and time, and adds it to your calendar. If the caller wants to move or cancel an appointment, it handles that too, by voice or by text.

How it handles the walk-in line

Barbershops don't run on appointments alone. A good AI receptionist works the walk-in queue: a customer can scan a QR code at the shop, join the line with just a name and number (nothing to download), and see their spot and rough wait on their phone. When their chair is next, they get a text — so they can step out for coffee without losing their place. A lobby TV can show the live “now serving / up next” board so the room stops interrupting you to ask “how much longer?”

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes — the better ones detect the language automatically. NextUp, the barbershop receptionist from Vozlia, hears Spanish and answers in Spanish with no menu and no “press 2.” It switches on its own.

Does it keep my shop's number?

You keep your existing number. There's no new hardware and no new line — calls forward to the assistant when you're busy or after hours, and you can set trusted regulars and family to ring straight through to your cell.

What it remembers

It captures every caller's name, number, and history automatically, and recognizes returning customers — so a regular who calls back weeks later is greeted by name with their usual service already known, instead of being a stranger every time.
