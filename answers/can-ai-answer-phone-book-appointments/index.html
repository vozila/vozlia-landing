How it works

Can AI answer my shop's phone and book appointments?

The short answer

Yes. An AI receptionist picks up your calls, talks to the caller in plain language, finds an open slot, books it, and syncs it to your calendar — and it can reschedule or cancel the same way. It works by voice on the phone and by text, around the clock.

How the booking actually happens

The caller says what they want (“Can I get a fade Saturday afternoon?”). The AI checks your real availability, offers a time, confirms it, and writes it to the calendar — no callback, no message for you to process later. If they need to move or cancel, they just say so, by voice or text, and it updates everything.

Does it work over text too, not just voice?

Yes. The same assistant answers on the channels your customers already use — phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram DM, and web chat — with the same booking tools behind all of them. If someone DMs you on Instagram today and calls next week, it's still one assistant that knows who they are.

What about walk-ins, not just appointments?

For a barbershop it also runs the walk-in line: customers self-join by QR code, see their place and wait time, and get a text when they're up. Appointments and the walk-in queue live in the same system.

Will it double-book me or book when I'm closed?

No — it books against your real availability and hours, and it respects standing rules you set (for example, “I don't work Mondays”). You can also route family and trusted regulars straight to your own phone and let the assistant handle everyone else.

What do I need to set up?

You keep your existing number — no new hardware, no new line. Calls forward to the assistant when you're busy or after hours. NextUp (from Vozlia) is built for barbershops and is up and running quickly; see vozlia.com.
