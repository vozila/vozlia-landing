Pricing

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

The short answer

It depends on whether it's AI-only or human-backed and how it's billed, but as a market benchmark: AI-only receptionists commonly run from roughly $25 to $95 per month, while human and hybrid services run higher — Smith.ai, for example, publishes an AI tier from about $95/month and human tiers from about $292.50/month, billed per call. For Vozlia's own pricing, see vozlia.com.

What you're paying for at each level

  • AI-only receptionists — a flat monthly rate for the AI to answer, book, and follow up. This is the lower-cost end of the market.
  • Human and hybrid services — you pay more because a person handles some or all calls, usually billed per call with a monthly plan and overage charges.

How is it usually billed — per call, per minute, or flat?

It varies. Some AI tools charge a flat monthly rate; hybrid services like Smith.ai bill per call (with plan caps and overage), which is more predictable than per-minute for long calls but adds up as volume grows. Read the billing model, not just the headline price.

Is a cheaper AI receptionist always the better deal?

Not necessarily. The cheapest tool that only takes messages can cost you more in walked-out bookings than a slightly pricier one that actually books the appointment and works the walk-in line. Match the tool to how your shop makes money.
