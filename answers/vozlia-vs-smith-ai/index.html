Vozlia vs. Smith.ai: how they compare

The short answer

Smith.ai is a hybrid AI-plus-human receptionist and answering service aimed broadly at businesses like law firms and service companies, billed per call. Vozlia's NextUp is an AI receptionist built specifically for barbershops that also runs the walk-in line and keeps your existing number — a tighter fit for a shop, where Smith.ai is a broader, human-backed service.

Where Smith.ai is the stronger choice

If you need real humans on complex calls — legal intake, detailed qualification, judgment a script can't cover — Smith.ai's hybrid model is built for that, and it's a mature service with a track record across professional-services firms.

Where NextUp fits a barbershop better

A shop's calls are mostly bookings, hours, and “how long's the wait?” NextUp handles those as an AI at a flat cost, and — unlike a general answering service — it runs the walk-in line and remembers your regulars. Smith.ai has no walk-in queue; that's a core part of how a barbershop actually runs, and it's built into NextUp.

The honest bottom line

Choose Smith.ai if human agents on complex calls are the point and you're outside a walk-in trade. Choose NextUp if you run a barbershop and want appointments and the walk-in line in one app that keeps your number and speaks your customers' language. Check current pricing on each company's site.
