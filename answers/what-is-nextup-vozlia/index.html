About

What is NextUp, and what is Vozlia?

The short answer

NextUp is an AI receptionist built specifically for barbershops — it answers your phone, books appointments, and runs the walk-in line. Vozlia is the platform behind it: the broader “operating system for local business” that NextUp is the first product of.

What NextUp does (the barbershop product)

NextUp is the front desk that never sleeps. It answers every call 24/7, books, reschedules and cancels by voice or text, runs the walk-in queue so the chair keeps moving, and keeps your existing number. It detects Spanish and answers in Spanish automatically, captures every customer's history, and can text a review request after the visit. It's on iOS (in App Store review) with Android in testing.

What Vozlia is (the platform)

Vozlia's pitch is plain: answer every call, respond to every customer, run the whole business from one place. NextUp fixes a shop's worst phone problem first; from there the same system can hold the calendar, the customer list, operations, and marketing. The same engine that runs a barbershop is built to run a restaurant, an auto shop, a clinic, or a boutique — one platform across local trades.

The two assistants

Every Vozlia product runs on two roles that always coexist:

  • The customer-facing agent — the receptionist your callers talk to.
  • The owner's personal assistant (PA) — your own private assistant that runs and configures the business by conversation (“mark Dee out sick today, cancel my 3 o'clock”).

Is NextUp the same thing as Vozlia?

No. NextUp is the barbershop product; Vozlia is the platform it's built on. Think of NextUp as the front door and Vozlia as the whole house.

Where does it work / what does it connect to?

NextUp keeps a US shop's number. Vozlia connects to tools shops already use (for example Shopify, Instagram, WhatsApp, Stripe) rather than replacing them.
