AI receptionist vs. a phone answering service: what's the difference?

The short answer

A traditional answering service is a room of human agents who pick up your calls, take a message, and email or text it to you. An AI receptionist answers the call itself and actually does the task — books the appointment, updates the calendar, joins someone to the walk-in line — 24/7, without waiting for a human agent to be free.

When a human answering service still makes sense

If most of your calls are genuinely complex — legal intake, nuanced medical triage, judgment calls a script can't cover — a human or hybrid service earns its higher price. That's the sweet spot for services like Smith.ai's live tiers.

When an AI receptionist is the better fit for a barbershop

Most barbershop calls are the same handful of things: “Are you open?”, “Can I get in at 3?”, “How long's the wait?”, “I need to move my appointment.” Those are exactly what an AI handles instantly and completely, for a flat cost, without a message sitting in an inbox waiting for you to act on it between clients. It also does things a message-taking service structurally can't: run the live walk-in queue and text the customer when their chair is up.

The thing an answering service doesn't give you at all

A message service hands you a note. NextUp (from Vozlia) also remembers the customer — recognizing returning callers and their usual service — and can request a review by text after the visit. You're not just catching the call; you're keeping the relationship.
