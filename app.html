How Vozlia uses Google Calendar

When a business owner connects their Google Calendar to Vozlia, we use it purely for scheduling on their behalf:

Write appointments (calendar.events)

Appointments booked in Vozlia are added to the staff member’s own Google Calendar, and updated or removed when a booking changes — so their personal calendar always reflects their Vozlia schedule.

Read availability (calendar.readonly)

Vozlia reads busy times so a staff member who is already booked — in Vozlia or on another tool that syncs to their Google Calendar — isn’t offered for a new appointment or walk-in, preventing double-booking.

Vozlia only uses calendar events and availability to run scheduling for that business. We do not sell this data, and access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the calendar.
