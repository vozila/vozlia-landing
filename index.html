Questions, answered

FAQ

Does NextUp work with my existing phone number? +
Yes. You forward your existing shop number to NextUp when you're busy — no port-out, no number change, no new SIM. Your customers keep calling the same number they always have.
Do I need to install hardware? +
No. NextUp is software-only. Set-up takes about three minutes from your phone — no headset, no PBX, no IT visit.
What does the AI sound like? +
Friendly and conversational, not a phone tree. On Pro and above, you can clone your own voice so customers hear you greet them when you can't pick up.
Can my customers tell it's AI? +
Most don't. It speaks naturally, handles interruptions, and switches between English and Spanish mid-call. We disclose it's a virtual assistant if asked directly.
Is the free tier really free? +
Yes. Free Backup is $0 forever — no credit card, no trial timer. You share a NextUp line so we keep costs at zero. Upgrade to Pro any time you want your own dedicated number.
What about my Booksy or Square account? +
Keep it. NextUp answers the calls and texts your booking platform can't — we hand new appointments back to your existing system. No migration.
How do I cancel? +
From the app in two taps. No phone call, no retention agent. Free Backup never charges you in the first place.
